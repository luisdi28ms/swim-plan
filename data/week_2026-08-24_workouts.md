## Workout 1 — Serie 1

1. 500 L — 500 free
2. 3x100 tec-nad VE — 3x100, alternating technique/swim, varying strokes (VE)
3. 6x50 (25 pat max vel – 25 suave) 20sp — 6x50 as 25 max-speed kick + 25 easy, 20s rest
4. 3 sin patas – 3 con patas — 3 reps without fins, 3 reps with fins (applies to the 6x50 above)
5. 4x50 prog opt — 4x50 progressive, optional (Opt)
6. 10x200, 20 o 30 sp — 10x200, 20 or 30s rest, broken down as:
   - Reps 1, 3, 5: paddles + fins, easy, focus on technique
   - Reps 2, 4, 6, 8: free swim (L)
   - Reps 7, 9: 25 stroke – 75 free
   - Rep 10: free swim (L)
7. 100 alfoje — 100 warm-down/easy

**Open question:** step 4 ("3 sin patas – 3 con patas") — I read this as describing the 6x50 in step 3 (half without fins, half with fins). Confirm that's the right pairing and not its own separate set.

## Workout 2 — Serie 2

1. 600 (200L – 100L pb) — 600 total, pattern of 200 free / 100 free with pull buoy (pb), repeated
2. 2x200 (50 pat flecha – 50L nad) patas — 2x200 kick set: 50 streamline kick (pat flecha) + 50 free swim
3. 8x15 vel VE — 8x15 at speed, varying strokes (VE)
4. Serie x4 (repeat the following block 4 times):
   - 200 L patas — 200 free, kick
   - 100 L — 100 free
   - 4x25 vel 10sp — 4x25 at speed, 10s rest
   - 100 suave — 100 easy
5. 100 afloje — 100 warm-down/easy

**Open question:** step 1, "600 (200L-100L pb)" — I read this as 2 rounds of (200 free + 100 free pull buoy) = 600 total. Confirm the round count.

## Workout 3 — Fondo (with Sunday/"Domingo" notes)

1. Sesión técnica — technique session (title/header for the day)
2. 600 L — 600 free
3. 3x200, each broken as: 100 technique of choice – 100 free applying what was practiced in the technique
4. 3x100 (25 pat flecha – 75L nad) patas — kick set: 25 streamline kick + 75 free swim, x3
5. 8x100 L, VL BL, focus on technique — long turns (VL), long strokes (BL):
   - 4 reps without fins/paddles
   - 4 reps with fins/paddles
6. 4x100 L: higher speed without losing technique, controlled breathing
7. 200 pb — 200 pull buoy
8. 100 alfoje — 100 warm-down/easy

**Coach's note on the 100s:** for the 100s, try to reduce average stroke count per 25 by at least 1–2 strokes.

**Sunday ("Domingo Fondo en entreno") notes — not a 4th workout, guidance for this training day:**
- Everyone does whatever best suits their nearest fondo (distance) event.
- Practice race-day breakfast; bring gels or whatever you normally use in competition.
- No extra equipment needed this day.
